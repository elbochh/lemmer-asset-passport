# Lemmer company context
Source: https://lemmer.com/en/
Retrieved: 25 September 2026

Lemmer supplies professional spraying equipment and rustproofing application equipment. Its website lists branches in Calgary, Mississauga and Terrebonne. The demo's sales catalogue is limited to the website's rustproofing category, while individual service assets may be equipment from other manufacturers.
