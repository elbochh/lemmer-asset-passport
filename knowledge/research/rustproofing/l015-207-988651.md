## Catalogue description

This all-metal undercoating gun is designed for the application of most bitumen/wax-based rustproofing coatings for soundproofing, gravel protection and undercoating.

1.75« diameter threads and 18.5 cm (7») suction hose fit most 1-litre (0.2G) bottles

The adjustment on the front of the gun allows you to control product flow and apply different textures.

The RP-50 includes a short 1.27cm (1/2 ») nozzle and a longer 23 CM (9″) wand for applying the coating to cavities, hollow beams, doors, etc.

A 23cm (9″)replacement tube is also included.

Maximum pressure 0.86 MPa (125 psi)
