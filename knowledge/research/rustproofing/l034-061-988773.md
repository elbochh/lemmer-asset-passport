## Catalogue description

This hose cover is the essential accessory for protecting your paint hoses (Airless, HVLP or conventional) against the accumulation of dust, dirt and overspray (overspray). It keeps your hoses in like-new condition and makes cleaning up after your work much easier.

Features and benefits :

Maximum protection : Prevents paint from drying directly on the hose, preventing it from becoming stiff or sticky.

Upper thickness : Made of robust polyethylene 6 mil, with excellent resistance to tearing and rubbing on the ground.

Practical diameter : Its 2 inches (5 cm) diameter allows most single or twin hoses to slide easily inside.

Time saving : Eliminates the need to scrub your hoses with harsh solvents at the end of the day; simply remove the soiled protection.

Technical specifications :

Diameter : 2 inches (50 mm).

Thickness : 6 mils (industrial quality).

Usage : Compatible with Airless, twin and compressed-air hoses.
