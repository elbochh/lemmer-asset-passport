Tip extension features:

WARNING - High pressure spray can be dangerous!
• Read spray pump operating manual and safety instructions.
• Maximum operating pressure of this extention is 3500 psi.
• Do not use this metal pole near any electrical or high tension wire, electrical
shock could cause serious injury or death.
• Never put your hand, fingers or body over the spray tip or a leak. Injection
could occur. In case of penetration, seek medical aid immediately, do not
treat as a simple cut! Be prepared to tell the doctor what fluid was injected.
• Do not bend pole. Discontinue use if pole leaks or is bent, or becomes
corroded. These conditions could cause dangerous leaks.
• Final clean-up , displace moisture with mineral spirits to avoid corrosion.
