# RP-1202 kit identification and preparation reference
Source: https://lemmer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Manual-RP-1202-L033-207814-L500-006.pdf
Revision: L500-006; no printed revision date identified on the two-page manual
Applicability: L033-207 (20 L) and L033-208 (60/205 L), as printed on page 1. Do not extend to other configurations without verification.
Reviewed: 26 September 2026; page 2 visually checked against its English/French diagram.

The diagram distinguishes the fluid-pressure gauge from the air-pressure gauge, a pump air inlet, gun air regulator, fluid flow control, mixing cap, fluid filter, suction filter and pulsation dampener. These labels identify components; they do not establish which component has failed.

For a report of low pressure, ask which gauge/measurement is meant, its reading and unit, and whether the issue occurs at rest or while spraying. Record whether the reported symptom is reduced flow, poor atomization or a pulsating jet. These are application intake questions based on the diagram, not manufacturer fault diagnoses.

Page 2 contains the manufacturer's assembly and operating procedure. Refer a qualified operator to the complete manual, including page 1 warnings, for preparation. This document does not provide a low-pressure fault-to-replacement table or justify replacing seals, valves or filters based only on the phrase “low pressure.”
